Bench Pressure

A Bayesian lab rush

Grants in. Papers out. Every plate should teach you something.

Each grant ticket arrives with a prior over hypotheses, drawn as bars. Pick experiments that split that belief well, read the plates, and publish the most probable hypothesis before the grant runs out. The posterior updates by exact Bayes' rule, and the truth is drawn from the prior you see.

This game needs a physical keyboard. On a phone or tablet you can read the rules and watch Gemini play, but you cannot play yourself.

The bench loop

  1. Plate rack: grab a clean petri dish.
  2. Strain freezer: tap Use to choose the grant (colour), hold Use to inoculate.
  3. Design bench: tap Use to cycle experiments, hold Use to prepare one.
  4. Incubator: grows in 6 s. Take it out soon after or it contaminates.
  5. Plate reader: hold Use. The grant's posterior bars update.
  6. Autoclave: hold Use to wash the dish. It returns to the rack.
  7. Writing desk: hold Use to write up the top hypothesis; carry the paper to the journal hatch.

Correct paper: 100 + time left + a bonus for using fewer experiments. Wrong paper: −60 retraction. Expired grant: −30. Contaminated plates go in the bin.

Round over

Bits gathered is the realised information: the KL divergence from each prior to its posterior, summed over every plate read. Design efficiency is the expected information gain of the experiments you prepared, divided by the best expected gain on offer at the moment you chose.
