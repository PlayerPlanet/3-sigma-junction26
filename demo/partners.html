We took part in five challenges at Junction 2026. Here is what we found that relates to each one. Measured numbers are in Proof.

AfterQuery · Break the Agent

Thank you. The black-box credit desk is our submission. Frontier agents score below a fair reference because they misprice information, and the exact grader shows where each dollar went. Six desks, 54 graded trials, regrade in under a second.

Verda

Thank you. Our Harbor evaluation VM ran on Verda CPU, and Gemma GRPO trained on a Verda V100 spot instance. Kimi K3.1 Pro and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash ran through Verda inference: on the desk Kimi scored 0.43 and DeepSeek 0.27, and both bought calibration data up to or near the 1,000-draw cap.

NVIDIA

Thank you. Nemotron 3 Ultra through NVIDIA NIM scored 0.01 on the black-box desk, overbuying calibration and then deciding poorly.

Modal

Thank you. Modal ran the CPU fan-out for our grader experiments (scripted policies over 400 seeds per cell), and hosts this demo with a live Gemma GPU endpoint.

Google DeepMind

Thank you. Gemini 4 Argon was the only agent we saw price its own calibration by simulation; that trial scored 1.0, though most Argon trials hit the time limit. Gemini 4 Carbon scored 1.0 on one desk. Gemini plays Bench Pressure live. Gemma 4 E2B trained with GRPO on our reward went from 0.00 to 0.28, and its transfer to other tasks is a bias towards gathering information.

Desk scores are mean rewards on the black-box credit desk (1 = the reference's expected value, 0 = declining everyone), from results/blackbox_v2/trials.csv.
